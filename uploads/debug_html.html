Topic:
Proposed Ordinance on Community Health and Preventive Care Programs
Category:
Health & Sanitation
Description
The City Government of Valenzuela invites public consultation on the proposed ordinance establishing barangay‑level preventive health programs. This measure seeks to strengthen early detection of lifestyle‑related diseases, expand vaccination coverage, and promote community awareness campaigns on nutrition and mental health. Through this ordinance, the city aims to institutionalize annual medical check‑ups for residents, improve access to essential medicines, and enhance coordination between barangay health stations and the City Health Office. The initiative also includes the creation of a centralized digital health record system to streamline patient tracking and ensure continuity of care. Citizens are encouraged to provide feedback on the proposed implementation framework, funding allocation, and community engagement strategies. All submissions will help refine the ordinance before its formal deliberation by the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
